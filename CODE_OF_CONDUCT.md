# Code of Conduct

Duskly follows the [Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for contributors with different levels of experience and different backgrounds. Be kind, assume good intent, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, doxxing, threats, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to `security@duskly.site`. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, block accounts, or take other reasonable action to protect the project community.
